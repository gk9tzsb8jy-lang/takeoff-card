    Chart conditions and limits

    • Paved, level, dry runway. Cowl flaps ½ open, 2800 RPM, 40 in. MAP.
    • Sea level to 8,000 ft pressure altitude, ISA −15°C to ISA +30°C.
    • Takeoff 3,000 to 4,750 lb; landing 3,000 to 4,513 lb (maximum landing weight). Headwind to 15 kt, tailwind to 5 kt.
    • Landing charts assume both throttles closed, full-stall touchdown and maximum braking. Ground roll is the same for normal and short-field landings; the short-field technique only shortens the air distance, by crossing the 50 ft barrier slower (68 to 82 KIAS instead of 76 to 90).
    • Accelerate-stop includes a 3-second recognition time. The chart value is multiplied by 0.93 (0° flaps) or 0.87 (25° flaps), as the chart notes direct.
    • Off-station runways (airports with a paved runway of 2,800 ft or more; all their paved runways are listed): US airports come entirely from the FAA's 28-day airport data, refreshed weekly, including lengths, true headings, field elevations, displaced thresholds and declared distances (TORA, ASDA, LDA). Airports outside the US come from OurAirports (public domain). Where no declared distances are published, takeoff uses full runway length and landing uses length minus any displaced threshold. Headings and winds are true, matching METARs; ATIS winds are magnetic.
    • KSPG runway data uses the declared distances: takeoff run available (TORA) for the over-50 ft distance, accelerate-stop available (ASDA) for accelerate-stop and landing distance available (LDA) for landing over 50 ft. They come from the FAA's 28-day airport data (cycle effective October 1, 2026). Verify them against the current Chart Supplement and NOTAMs.
    • Charts are unfactored. The POH warns against using values extrapolated beyond the chart, so the card flags any input outside these limits.

      Limits used

      • Maximum ramp 4,773 lb, takeoff 4,750 lb, landing 4,513 lb, zero fuel 4,470 lb standard (POH 2.11).
      • Forward CG limit 82.0 in at 3,400 lb and below, 86.7 in at 4,250 lb, 90.6 in at 4,750 lb, straight line between. Aft limit 94.6 in at all weights (POH 2.13, Figure 6-15).
      • Arms: front seats 85.5 in, center seats 119.1 in (aft facing; 118.1 forward facing), rear seats 157.6 in, forward baggage 22.5 in, aft baggage 178.7 in, fuel 93.6 in. Datum 78.4 in ahead of the wing leading edge at the inboard edge of the fuel tank.
      • Start, taxi and runup fuel: 23 lb (POH Figure 6-9). Takeoff weight is fuel at engine start less this allowance.
      • The burn line from takeoff to landing is checked every 50 lb, since the forward limit moves with weight. Gear retraction moves the moment by only −32 in-lb, which the POH calls insignificant.
